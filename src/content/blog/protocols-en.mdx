## Evolution of Modern Proxy Protocols

In the ever-evolving landscape of network circumvention, proxy protocols have transitioned from basic symmetric encryption to zero-copy streaming, TLS certificate borrowing, and brutal UDP-based congestion control.

### 1. Shadowsocks (SS): Minimalist Architecture for IEPL Lines

Shadowsocks provides symmetric stream encryption with minimal CPU overhead. While easily detectable over raw public networks due to entropy heuristics, it remains the gold standard inside dedicated **IEPL / IPLC enterprise tunnels** where public filtering does not occur.

### 2. VLESS (XTLS-Reality): The Summit of Camouflage and Throughput

Developed by the Xray and Sing-box communities, VLESS removes redundant encryption wrappers and leverages **Reality certificate borrowing**. It masquerades proxy traffic under authentic TLS certificates of major tech companies (Apple, Microsoft, Cloudflare), completely neutralizing active MITM probing.

### 3. Hysteria 2: The Brutal UDP Breakthrough for Lossy Networks

Built upon modern QUIC/UDP with custom Brutal congestion control, Hysteria 2 refuses to throttle speed upon encountering packet loss. It delivers uninterrupted 4K video playback even under severe 20%~40% cross-border packet degradation.
