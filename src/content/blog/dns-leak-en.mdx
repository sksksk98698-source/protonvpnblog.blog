## Understanding DNS Leaks

Even with active proxies, unencrypted UDP port 53 DNS queries routed to local ISP servers can completely expose your browsing destination and location.

### Core Defense Mechanisms

1. **Deploying DNS over HTTPS (DoH)**: Encrypt DNS queries inside standard TLS packets (`https://1.1.1.1/dns-query` or `https://8.8.8.8/dns-query`).
2. **Mitigating WebRTC IP Leaks**: Disabling browser STUN leaks that bypass virtual network interfaces.
